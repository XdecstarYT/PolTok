# PolTok "What if…" series: post copy

Every number comes from real data: the VEC's 2022 Victorian results, the latest polls on Wikipedia, the AEC referendum results, and the UK's 2024 turnout. Where a reel is a what-if, it is arithmetic on real votes, not a prediction, and it says so on screen.

**Alt text:** A 3D hall with 100 figures, each 1% of enrolled voters. 88 glow green to show Victoria's 2022 turnout, then they dim to 60 to match the UK's 59.7% voluntary turnout. A counter shows 1,243,634 fewer Victorian voters. It ends asking "Should voting be compulsory?".

## What if the Voice had passed? `poltok-what-if-voice.mp4` (38s)

Facts only: the proposed law (Constitution Alteration (Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Voice) 2023) and the AEC result. The reel takes no side, so keep the post copy neutral too.

**Instagram / TikTok**
> What if the Voice had passed? 🇦🇺
>
> What it would have been: an advisory body that could make representations to Parliament and government. No veto, and Parliament decides how it works.
>
> What happened: No 60.06% · Yes 39.94%. Every state voted No; the ACT voted Yes.
> Only 8 of 45 referendums have ever passed.
>
> Would you vote the same way today? 👇
> #auspol #voicereferendum #referendum #australia #history

**X**
> 3 years ago, Australia voted on the Voice.
>
> It would have been an advisory body, with no veto.
> Result: No 60.06% · Yes 39.94%. All 6 states No.
> Only 8 of 45 referendums have ever passed.
>
> Would you vote the same way today?

**Alt text:** Illustrative documents land on a desk: a referendum ballot paper, the proposed constitutional change, and the result. On-screen text explains the proposed advisory body (it could make representations, had no veto, and Parliament would set how it worked), then the result of No 60.06% to Yes 39.94%, with all six states voting No and the ACT voting Yes, then "8 of 45 referendums have passed" and the question "Would you vote the same way today?".
