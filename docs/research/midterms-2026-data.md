## Balance of power before the election

- **Senate:** 53 R, 45 D, 2 I (both caucus with D, so 47 in the Dem caucus). 35 seats are up: 33 Class 2 seats plus the **FL and OH specials**. Republicans are defending 22 of them and Democrats 13. **Dems need a net gain of 4** to reach 51, because VP Vance breaks ties.
- **Senate appointees:** Ashley Moody (FL, special), Jon Husted (OH, special), Darline Graham (SC; Lindsey Graham died in 2026 and his sister was appointed July 2026, then won the GOP runoff), and Alan Armstrong (OK; Markwayne Mullin resigned to lead DHS, and Armstrong is not running).
- **House:** 218 R, 214 D, 1 I (Kevin Kiley, CA, who left the GOP in Mar 2026 and still caucuses with R, so the GOP conference is 219), and 2 vacancies (FL-20, last held by D; TX-23, last held by R). 218 is a majority. Dems need **net +3 vs. their 2024 total of 215**, or 4 more than the 214 currently seated.
- **Redistricting:** mid-decade maps were redrawn in TX (+5 R-leaning), CA (Prop 50, about D+4), FL (May 2026, about R+3), OH, NC (NC-1), LA, and AL; UT has a court map with a new D seat; MO kept its 2022 map. Cook estimates the new lines are worth a net of about **R+4** (Yahoo/Cook).
- **Governors:** 26 R and 24 D; 36 seats are up (18 R-held, 18 D-held).
